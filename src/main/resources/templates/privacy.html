Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 1, 2026

Fidelity Forge (Wooshin Studio, "we", "our") operates the mobile game Fidelity Forge. This policy explains how we collect and use your information.

1. Information We Collect

  • Google Play account identifier (for login and account linking)
  • Game progress data (stage records, in-game currency, etc.)
  • In-app purchase receipt (for VIP subscription verification)
  • Advertising ID (GAID) and device information (for ad services)
  • App usage statistics and crash logs (for service improvement)
  • Access timestamp (for service operation and fraud prevention)

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Creating and maintaining your game account
  • Saving game progress and syncing across devices
  • Verifying in-app purchases and granting benefits
  • Preventing fraud and ensuring service stability

3. Data Retention

We retain your data until you request account deletion. Upon deletion request, your data is removed immediately, except where retention is required by applicable law.

4. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data with the following third parties to operate our service:

  • Google Play – in-app purchase and subscription processing
  • Google AdMob – ad delivery (collects advertising ID and device info). Additional ad networks may be integrated in the future.
  • Google Firebase – analytics, crash reporting (Crashlytics), and authentication. Usage may expand in future updates.
  • Law enforcement – when required by applicable law

Please refer to each provider's privacy policy for details on their data practices.

5. Your Rights

You may request access, correction, deletion, or restriction of your personal data at any time by contacting us.
